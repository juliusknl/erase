# Broker foundation and initial pilot — 30 September 2026

The useful asset is the combination of controller identity, matching requirements,
request scope, maintained official evidence and lessons from outcomes. A large
list of names alone is easy to reproduce. Public-source research does not establish
that any particular person appears in a database, or that a removal will succeed.

## What this version supports

`catalog/mvp-cohort.yml` declares the pilot explicitly; run the catalog audit for
current email and guided-form counts. `catalog/email-routes.yml` connects reviewed guides to exact
recipients, residence applicability, approved identifiers, request text, expected
verification and dated evidence. The broader research directory stays available,
including incomplete entries. No claim of complete market coverage is made.

The selection follows personal exposure and sensitivity. US providers holding
Europeans' professional data are included. Headquarters are not an eligibility
criterion. Named contact data, home addresses, location histories and links between
identifiers are priority areas. These are risk-based editorial priorities, not
allegations that the named companies misuse data or measured exposure probabilities.
The manifest preserves important frontiers even when their workflows need more work.

An initial email workflow is permitted when the official provider accepts that
request for the selected resident, no documented required field is omitted, and
any additional verification is explicit. Unspecified later verification is not
represented as a fully automatic deletion. Guided forms have reviewed official
routes; some dynamic forms still have incomplete field checklists, explicitly
shown in their instructions. Later authenticated steps are completed by the person.

  suppression effect rather than claiming complete erasure.

All exact source links, retrieval dates and short excerpts are in the workflow
records. Evidence has a 90-day maximum age; age is measured from its check date,
not the date an installation first reads the catalog. No real requests were made
during this public-source review.

## User workflow

Open Campaign plan, choose residence and optionally allow the saved postal address
for workflows that need it. Update the previews and review the actual messages,
including their destinations and suppression choices. Approve once. The controller
validates the published workflow evidence, replenishes drafts and dispatches under
the selected daily limit. Initial profile requests need a saved email used outside
the app; a fresh dedicated mailbox alone is not treated as a useful matching key.

Every send rechecks the exact approved payload and recipient, current instructions,
profile, connection, case state and daily budget. Uncertain sends are never replayed
automatically. Completed cases and workflow aliases do not generate duplicate initial
requests. New workflows or changed request text need a new preview; unchanged
approved workflows can continue. An address change pauses the address-enabled plan.

Existing installations must review this changed campaign contract once. The software
does not silently convert old generic-email permission into postal disclosure or a
different recipient. The preview can adopt a researched recipient for an unsent case;
it does not reroute an already submitted request.

Forms remain in Quick Wins. ContactOut, ZoomInfo and Lusha were added with matching
and verification instructions; old completed cases remain done. Checking a form off
records user completion, independently of broker-reported deletion evidence.

SalesIntel and RevenueBase now use explicitly supplied work email and employer;
SciLeads uses its documented email alternative. The 11 further guided routes are
Bookyourdata, Forager, Enlyft, Wiza, SalesQL, RelPro, UpLead, Instantly, Amplemarket,
RampedUp and Explorium. Their requirements are read directly from the reviewed
dossiers, including incomplete-field and suppression-only caveats. This is not
11 new tested submissions. An initial recipe never silently supplies guessed
employment details or sends the optional fields to unrelated brokers.

## Reproducible acceptance and remaining work

Run `uv run erasure-catalog audit --require-pilot`. This checks the declared pilot's
guide status, sources, recipient/field/coverage consistency, freshness and form
associations. `--require-mvp` additionally requires whole-inventory accounting and
implementation of reviewed automatic-email decisions. It currently fails, as does
the research-only `--require-complete` gate, while the wider inventory is unfinished.
Structural success is not an independent source audit or real-world outcome evidence.

The integration tests use synthetic identities and a fake Gmail sender to exercise
residence/field permission, recipe changes, exact previews, duplicate prevention,
controller replenishment and budget enforcement. They do not count as live deletion.

The [30 September expansion review](research/workflow-expansion-2026-09-30.md)
records the next 13 email workflows, Growbots form routing, HubSpot field checks,
and unresolved forms. The audit also exports every remaining primary-email guide
without an executable workflow, including its matching and verification caveats.

The [full email-backlog assessment](research/email-workflow-review-2026-09-30.md)
then assesses all 125 remaining primary-email guides: 23 more initial workflows
were implemented and 102 retain specific matching, regional, verification or
controller blockers. It distinguishes current public-page checks from review of
existing dated dossiers. The audit includes next steps and official source dates;
this assessment is not a claim of end-to-end validation for all 125.

Before a broad public release, run this cohort on consenting users' real requests,
record verification burden and actual outcomes, review no-match scope, and extend
the same standards to the high-exposure frontiers in the manifest. Browser automation,
device identifiers and replies requiring new disclosures remain unfinished work.
There is no substantiated promise of hundreds of removals or ten minutes per week yet.
