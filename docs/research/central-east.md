# Central/eastern Europe and Baltic review — 2026-09-29

These are public-source instruction reviews, not submitted requests or deletion tests.
All seven routes remain manual because they concern regulated credit information,
sensitive identifiers, personal authentication or record-specific eligibility.

| Guide | Main operational distinction |
| --- | --- |
| BISZ Hungary | Free personal KHR report; government e-ID or financial-institution access. Positive-history choices do not erase defaults. |
| Creditinfo Lithuania | Controller/processor roles differ; authenticated communication is required, but exact remote verification is not published. |
| Creditinfo Estonia | General rights contact differs from digitally signed payment-default complaints. Closing a personal portal account is not credit erasure. |
| SBCB Slovakia | Banking controller, with separate CRIF processing; qualified signature supports electronic handling. |
| NBCB Slovakia | Separate non-bank controller; consent, legitimate-interest and statutory register purposes require different treatment. |
| CREFO Latvia | Free authenticated access; signed disputes need reasons/evidence. Postal delivery can cost extra. |
| KIB Latvia | Browser-rendered public policy resolves an apparent empty page; email rights route recommends secure signature. |

Evidence, exact destinations, fields and remaining unknowns are in
`catalog/research/central-east.yml`. The Latvian regulator's complete two-license
roster is recorded in the discovery inventory; this is not an all-sector Latvian
broker census. Other country discoveries are narrow source-led candidates, not
claims of national exhaustiveness.

  electronic-signature conditions, plus their one-page erasure forms. Phone/email

  text prevails; general rights and specific default-dispute email addresses differ.

  Isolated public Chromium rendering loaded the actual policy with entity number,
  rights email, signature recommendation and DPO contact. No account was opened,
  no CAPTCHA completed and no identity data entered. The regulator lists a qualifying
  shareholder; do not infer sole or ultimate ownership from that listing.
- Database sizes are unknown rather than guessed from country populations, numbers
  of lenders, credit agreements or group-wide marketing statistics.
