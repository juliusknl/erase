# Northern, DACH and Central/Eastern European broker research

Checked 2026-09-28. This shard supplies 39 explicitly scoped guides, not an exhaustive European broker census. No requests were sent, accounts opened, private profiles consulted or outcomes tested. Publication dates are recorded where the source states one; crawl timestamps are not publication dates. Empty required-field lists mean unknown, not that no identifiers are required. Parent ownership and scale remain explicit unknowns unless independently supported.

## Scope and decisions

The guides cover address marketing/enrichment, professional contacts, advertising identifiers and credit-information access. They do not turn a credit access form, fraud freeze, marketing suppression or website-cookie control into a blanket deletion promise. Country companies sharing a multinational website are not inferred to share a controller or removal route. D&B and Creditsafe global domains are reserved to the main shard; their national entities below remain pending unless that shard establishes exact coverage.

The 39-guide publication is an evidence threshold, not a numeric stopping criterion. The inventories below preserve every encountered source-list lead, including unresolved ones. This pass is **not complete for every candidate**: the outstanding controller/product checks are a research backlog and do not count as verified coverage. CEE work established Poland/Czechia routes; Slovakia, Hungary, Baltics, Balkans, Ukraine, Moldova, Belarus and Iceland remain regional gaps. Switzerland's study is historic and cannot establish current market participation.

All 39 routes remain manual or blocked because of national identifiers, postal matching, authentication, suppression-only effects, incomplete matching instructions or product-scope uncertainty. This conservative mode does not claim that every published email route requires ID. Vainu, Goava, BizMachine and GEMINI DIRECT publish email rights routes, but current matching/verification follow-up is not tested.

Notable identity checks: AdressMonster now identifies G.I.G OOD (Bulgarian UIC 208570125), unlike the inventory's old B2B Monster UG. Its own page has inconsistent acquisition-year and scale figures, so neither was adopted. Swedish Checknode AB and Upplysning Checknode AB have different organisation numbers. The Intelligence Company permit number and its public policy number differ. Bislab's Norwegian policy does not automatically cover its Swedish company. KSV1870 Information and the association are not a single controller. ABIS updates existing addresses and explicitly denies selling new-customer address lists. Fonecta subscriber-directory removal is routed to the telecom operator, unlike its professional database requests.

[Norwegian Datatilsynet's agency list](https://www.datatilsynet.no/personvern-pa-ulike-omrader/kredittvurdering/virksomheter-som-kredittvurderer/) (updated 2025-10-30) expressly is not exhaustive. [Posti privacy](https://www.posti.fi/fi/asiakaspalvelu/ehdot-ja-selosteet/tietosuoja) and [target-group terms](https://www.posti.fi/kohderyhmapalveluiden-tuote-ehdot) identify a remaining marketing-address workflow to resolve: Posti Jakelu versus other group companies and statutory postal records must not be collapsed. Other Finnish credit suppliers and Iceland require further source-led discovery.

### Germany and Austria

Robinson suppression is not proof that each broker erased its record.

Bisnode Schweiz, Schweizer Verband Creditreform, Intrum, Easymonitoring, and Wys Muller remain current-identity/route checks. BusinessMonitor / Novertur may fit professional information, not necessarily credit reference. [Current EDOEB guidance](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en/credit-and-collection) distinguishes credit information, collection and member/statutory registers; ZEK/IKO are not ordinary marketing brokers.

[SDV board membership](https://sdv-dialogmarketing.ch/ueber-uns/unser-verband/) surfaces AZ Direct AG (guide), Post CH (pending), and agencies/service organisations. DataCrea, Bühler & Bühler, Barbara Dürst, ZHAW, NEXIMO, Wirz and one marketing are not promoted merely because they are sector members: independent database brokering is unestablished. KünzlerBachmann Directmarketing and SAZ require complete current policy/identity checks.

### Poland and Czechia

All four have access-first guides; account/form details still need manual review. The separately operated BIK is a pending bank-credit registry lead, not inferred coverage from BIG InfoMonitor.

[Czech regulator's 2022 information response](https://uoou.gov.cz/urad/povinne-zverejnovane-informace/svobodny-pristupu-k-informacim/informace-poskytnute-na-zadost/informace-poskytnute-na-zadost-v-roce-2022/poskytnuta-informace-dne-1-zari-2022) helped discover SOLUS/CRIF. Their current official notices/forms support scoped access guides, but CRIF kolikmam remains needs-research because the landing page failed and the available form is older. CBCB/BRKI and CNCB/NRKI require separate controller decisions; SOLUS positive-register objection is not negative-register erasure. BizMachine has a professional-database guide.
