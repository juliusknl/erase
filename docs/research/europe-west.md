# Western Europe broker research

Checked 2026-09-28. Countries: France, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Spain, Portugal, Italy.

## Status and limits

The companion catalog contains 42 records: 17 instructions-reviewed and 25 needs-research. Every outcome is untested; no forms, emails, identity uploads, private records, production databases or external writes were used. All routes remain manual or blocked. The discovery ledger below is broader than the actionable catalog and remains open: this is not a claim that every broker in these countries has been found or fully audited.

The eight areas are identity/parent, jurisdiction and data relevance, request route, required information and verification, actual coverage/retention, automation safety, dated evidence, and scale. Unknown ownership, identifier requirements, route scope and scale are explicit. A null scale means no retained supported claim, not small size. No prevalence claim says the user appears in a database.

Instructions-reviewed means a public route and its stated scope were read, not successful deletion, a completed legal audit, or a guarantee that no further verification is needed. A candidate may have an accessible generic privacy policy but remain blocked because that policy only covers website visitors. Consumer directories use people_search, reference identity services identity_risk, and multichannel consumer-list providers consumer_marketing after the schema expansion; mixed business-directory activity remains professional.

## Discovery method and boundaries

Official trade-association member lists, broker/product notices, telephone-directory disclosures, regulator material and credit-information registries were used alongside company-specific search. Membership is a discovery lead, not proof of brokerage. Existing main-catalog guides (including Kompass, Criteo, Kaspr, Cognism, Acxiom Germany and LiveRamp UK) were not duplicated.

Sources checked:

- [Alliance Digitale / DMA France members](https://www.dma-france.org/membres/): broad mixed membership; relevant data-provider leads listed below. The whole association is not classified as brokers.
- [ASEDIE association and committees](https://www.asedie.es/es/la-asociacion), [member directory](https://www.asedie.es/es/nuestros-asociados): Spanish commercial-information discovery; member logos require further entity-level review.
- [AMD Portugal members](https://www.amd.pt/associados-amd): includes advertisers, charities and service providers, not just data intermediaries.
- [ANCIC commercial-information directory](https://www.informativaprivacyancic.it/imprese-iscritte.aspx): finite named Italian roster retained below, with current entity changes unresolved where appropriate.
- [DDMA postal-marketing meeting](https://ddma.nl/kennisbank/terugblik-post-meetup-op-14-mei/): Whooz/Postfilter discovery; this was not an exhaustive DDMA membership audit.
- [FEDMA national associations](https://www.fedma.org/dma/): routing source, not an exhaustive broker register.
- Local CC0 discovery inventory `catalog/inventory/europe-discovery.json`, pinned datenananfragen revision `c314ad2ca8e0c4992f1130bdecd1b5fc51313a28`: candidate-only secondary discovery, never current contact verification.

### Benelux: pending discovery ledger and caveats

Black Tiger's current notice describes passive identity validation and explicitly distinguishes it from direct-marketing profiling. Its historic CMX consumer product should not be treated as a current available marketing database. The [Belgian regulator's 2024 notice](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/burger/gba-sanctioneert-gegevensbeheerder-black-tiger-belgium-wegens-gebrek-aan-transparantie) reports destruction of CMX and cessation of delivery; appeal disposition was not established here.

Freedelity roles vary: retailer transaction processing, jointly controlled contact reference data, and own-controller purposes must be separated. The [regulator's 2025 update](https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/citoyen/actualites/2025/12/08/dossier-freedelity-l-apd-donne-plus-de-temps-a-freedelity-et-ses-enseignes-clientes) concerns an extended compliance deadline, not proof that an individual deletion has occurred.

Whooz's current homepage redirects to Whize/TDA Analytics, while the product notice names Whooz BV. The guide records the notice controller and this identity gap. EDM visitor-policy rights must not be assumed to cover its reference datasets. Smart Profile's current product controller/workflow remains unresolved; smartprofile.net is a different business.

Luxembourg coverage includes Editus plus existing/shared multinational candidates. This is not proof that no further domestic providers exist. Creditsafe's [Luxembourg-labelled rights page](https://www.creditsafe.com/lu/en/more/about-us/gdpr/data-subject-access-request.html) names Creditsafe Belgium and describes five-year marketing suppression. Resolve regional routing and retain minimal suppression records as distinct from erasure; coordinate the shared domain with its existing guide.

## Request-safety findings

SLS Data publishes a DPO address visually reversed in extracted text; the guide records the readable address and keeps handling manual. It separately retains opposition records. FCR requests contact and, where relevant, business identifiers. Sirdata cookie rights require a cookie identifier and device-control declaration, not simply an email. Weborama likewise needs its advertising identifier. Informa Portugal calls for identity evidence. MEDIAPOSTE's monetisation charter forwards requests to the controller/partners and is not proof of universal removal.

ASNEF, Experian BADEXCUG, CRIF, CTC, Experian Italy and commercial risk-reporting services must not be included in indiscriminate marketing-deletion sending. Access first, inspect the actual record, then seek justified correction, restriction or erasure. A marketing suppression request does not erase accurate credit history.

Public forms were inspected only to the extent possible without submission. Fields behind authentication, conditional branches or later steps remain unknown. Identity documents should never be attached speculatively. Retained routes and references must be rechecked before use.

## Validation

The shard is JSON-formatted YAML for reliable quoting and validates with `KnowledgeCatalog`. Each record supplies all schema areas, HTTPS evidence dated 2026-09-28, an explicit review status, and an untested outcome. Cross-shard domain collision validation is required during integration. No California registration is used as the sole basis for western-European relevance.
