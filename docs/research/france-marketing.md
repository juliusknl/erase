# French marketing and professional-data review — 2026-09-29

Public research only: no mailbox, identity entry, request submission or deletion
test. Guides and explicit inventory associations are additive; they grant no
sending consent.

## Operational findings

- Avanci has campaign-specific rights handling, not universal group deletion.
  Its processor notice distinguishes suppression from requests passed onward.
  The mes-datas portal needs a campaign code; its platform DPO and campaign DPO
  are different addresses.
- Nomination's full decision-maker policy was recovered from the link in the
  current privacy page. The old indexed PDF redirects to that page. The current
  PDF is dated 2025-10-10, although its upload path is April2026. All11 pages were
  text-extracted and read. Its printed lookup address returned404; current site
  navigation provided a working work-email lookup page. No private link requested.
- HOSPIMEDIA Nominations is a separate healthcare-professional directory, not
  Nomination.fr and not a patient-record broker. Its FAQ gives a specific DPO-copy
  instruction that generic single-recipient dispatch does not implement.
- Valiuz's public login page documents an email-link flow. Combined-profile
  consent withdrawal, retailer records and browser-cookie choices have different
  scopes; one completion must not imply all three are erased.
- Critère Direct explicitly describes acting on opposition within its services
  and forwarding it to independent controllers.
- DataOnDemand's product offering establishes research relevance, but its
  website-contact notice does not establish the product-data removal procedure.
- WebRivage explicitly identifies Bonjour Data as its product. Its corporate and
  Bonjour Data notices remain website-scoped; product matching and inconsistent
  ID instructions need further resolution. Both are one research guide, not two
  independent deletion targets.
