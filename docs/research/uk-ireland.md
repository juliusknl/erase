# UK and Ireland public broker research

Checked 2026-09-28. The companion `catalog/research/uk-ireland.yml` contains 28 individually assessed guides, including unresolved workflows. These are public instructions, not evidence that any person appears in a database or that any removal succeeded. No requests, forms, messages, private profiles or live databases were accessed. No parent-company-wide removal is inferred.

## Method and bounded discovery

Discovery used regulator material, named-partner lists, source-product lists and official regional notices, rather than selecting a target count. The boundary is the named sources below, not every possible UK/Irish broker. Expanding one partner list indefinitely would create an unbounded web crawl; the explicit frontier below preserves newly discovered candidates without pretending they were reviewed.

The [ICO sector investigation](https://ico.org.uk/media/action-weve-taken/2618470/investigation-into-data-protection-compliance-in-the-direct-marketing-data-broking-sector.pdf) and [ICO broker-services guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/organisations-using-marketing-services-of-data-brokers/) establish the sector and distinguish list supply, enrichment, tracing and suppression. Historical investigation contacts are not treated as current rights destinations.

The [DLG survey notice](https://www.surveys.co.uk/docs/PrivacyPolicy_Surveys.pdf), updated 17 September 2026, supplies the first finite partner set. DLG's Surveys, Worthi, Consumer Lifestyles and ThePrizeFinder are one controller guide. Separate guides cover DM Data, MyOffers, PDV, Sagacity, CACI, Acxiom UK, Kinesso, LexisNexis/Tracesmart and GBG. Experian and LiveRamp already have guides in the main catalog. Sky, BT, ScottishPower and the Consumer Data Research Centre are identified customers or research recipients, not automatically independent brokers. This source does not justify a group-wide DM Ltd request.

The [Sagacity/My Data Choices notice](https://www.mydatachoices.co.uk/Privacy), updated 30 April 2026, supplies a second finite set: Zeta, InfoSum, Epsilon, LiveRamp, Eyeota, Acxiom, CACI, Experian, Kinesso, Equifax, LexisNexis, Datatrac, UKPhonebook, GBG and Conexance are now covered by new or existing guides. Medialab and RAPP are agency relationships, not established independent consumer databases on this evidence. XChannelMarketing links to Horizon CDP and remains a pending controller/product assessment. Meta and Samsung are platform/client relationships, not newly verified independent brokers. The remaining named insurers, charities, utilities, retailers, travel operators and financial clients are not classified as brokers merely because they buy services.

The [Data8 source-product directory](https://www.data-8.co.uk/data-sources/) supplies a third finite set. TPS, CTPS and MPS are preference infrastructure; they are not blanket deletion targets. Mortascreen, the Bereavement Register and the National Deceased Register are specialist deceased-person suppression products whose controllers and applicability need separate review. Business Suppression File, Re-Mover, GAS, GAS Reactive, Experian Absolute Movers/Contacts and DBS Purifi are products, not additional legal entities inferred from their labels. Data8, Sagacity, Experian and DBS have guide coverage, without asserting every product is included. Royal Mail NCOA, PAF, Multiple Residence, Not Yet Built, Eircode, Ordnance Survey AddressBase and BT OSIS need source-correction or specialist treatment; an address dataset does not necessarily identify a living person. USPS is outside the regional scope.

Supplemental official discovery covered 192.com and UKPhonebook directories, DataHQ business data, Market Location (also independently named in the [UK Business Data Survey notice](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-business-data-survey-2024-information-for-survey-participants/uk-business-data-survey-2024-privacy-notice)), 118 Information, TransUnion, Equifax, Vision-net/SoloCheck, Business.ie and Ireland's Central Credit Register. The CCR is explicitly a statutory credit system, not a commercial marketing broker. Business.ie now presents Bizmaps; an older public-library reference to DataIreland is not used to assert present An Post ownership.

## Guide dispositions

Each YAML record addresses identity, regional/data relevance, removal route, identifiers/verification, actual coverage, automation boundary, dated official evidence and scale. Unestablished scale is `null`: advertising audience counts, aggregated demographics and credit records are not comparable unique-person deletion totals.

| Records | Result and important boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Sagacity, CACI, DLG, DM Data, Data8, DBS, DataHQ, Epsilon/Abacus, Datatrac, PDV | Reviewed rights or suppression routes; retained suppression records and client/controller scope remain explicit. UK-specific requests are manual. |
| Kinesso, Eyeota, Zeta | Product-specific web rights paths; browser/cookie identifiers and email-hash audiences are distinct. No affiliate-wide completion is claimed. |
| 192.com, UKPhonebook | Reviewed listing suppression/removal forms with documented matching fields; removal is not source-register erasure. UKPhonebook explicitly requires the individual to act. |
| Conexance/CHOREOGRAPH SAS | Current French programme notice and exact privacy contact reviewed; processor requests may be forwarded to members. UK relevance comes from Sagacity's partner list and remains uncertain. |
| Acxiom UK | Current notice describes aggregated product information; individual product-record deletion route is unresolved. B2B newsletter opt-out is not substituted. |
| InfoSum | Current company privacy and clean-room product materials do not establish an independently held consumer broker database. Client-controlled datasets need the responsible controller. |
| TransUnion, Equifax, GBG, LexisNexis Risk, CRIF Vision-net, Central Credit Register | Specialist credit/identity/public-record workflows. Access, correction and purpose-specific objection must not be marketed as automatic deletion of valid regulated records. |
| Market Location, 118 Information, MyOffers, Business.ie/Bizmaps | Official identity/relevance evidence found, but substantive current product rights route or policy content could not be fully retrieved. Blocked pending research, not guessed email addresses. |

All records have `tested_outcome: not_tested`. No record enables `email_minimal`. UK-only email routes cannot use the existing template's assertion of EEA residence. Unknown fields do not mean no identity checks: they mean a minimum checklist was not established publicly.

## Source quality and unresolved questions

Each guide links its own official evidence. Particularly important limitations:

- LexisNexis's official business-services PDF remains dated 2021; rechecking its current product controller and procedure is required.
- Market Location's page embeds an external policy API whose substantive document was not retrievable; a sales enquiry address is not relabeled a rights route.
- MyOffers' current policy returned retrieval errors; DLG's current notice establishes the separate controller, not a working erasure destination.
- Business.ie exposes limited policy content and current Bizmaps branding; historical DataIreland links do not prove continuity of records or ownership.
- 118 Information's official policy wrapper establishes limited identity information, not a verified consumer workflow.
- Zeta's notice contains separate website/service effective dates and separate LiveIntent/Disqus policies. Eyeota's ownership does not extend its request to every Dun & Bradstreet dataset.
- Conexance's programme processor status, InfoSum's client-controlled environment and PDV's processor role are retained rather than rewritten as autonomous controllers for all data.
